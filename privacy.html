Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. Introduction

CONNECTA is a social and marketplace platform designed to help people connect, share content, communicate and discover products. This policy explains how personal information may be collected, used and protected when using CONNECTA.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, CONNECTA may process information such as:

  • Account information, including your email address and profile details.
  • Profile photographs and other content you choose to upload.
  • Posts, stories, videos, comments and messages.
  • Marketplace listings and interactions with sellers.
  • Technical information needed to operate and maintain the platform.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide account access, display content, support messaging, operate marketplace features, maintain platform security and improve the user experience.

4. Storage and Third-Party Services

CONNECTA uses Firebase services for authentication and database functionality, and Cloudinary for certain media uploads. These providers process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Sharing and Visibility

Content you publish or information you add to public profiles and marketplace listings may be visible to other users. Private account information should not be published as public content. We do not promise that every interaction or message is end-to-end encrypted.

6. Data Security

We aim to use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices

You should review the information you publish and keep your account credentials secure. Requests concerning access, correction or deletion of personal information can be directed to the CONNECTA team.

8. Children's Privacy

CONNECTA is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as CONNECTA develops. Any revised version will be published on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use the CONNECTA contact page.
